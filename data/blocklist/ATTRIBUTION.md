# Offensive-word lists (suggestion filter)

From "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words" (LDNOOBW),
https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words
— CC BY 4.0 (FSF: compatible with all versions of the GNU GPL).
Adaptation: single-word entries only, lowercased, sorted. There is no Ukrainian
list upstream; uk.txt reuses the Russian list. es, fr, it, pt, pl, nl (0.7.19)
from the same lists.

Used only to keep words out of suggestions and autocorrect targets (like
Gboard's "Block offensive words", on by default). Typing them is never blocked.
